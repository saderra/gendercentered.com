The questions everyone argues about

Asked honestly. Answered plainly.

Fairness in Sports

Do trans women hold an advantage in women's sports? The research is messier than either side wants you to know, and the answer changes depending on the sport, the athlete, and the age of transition.

You deserve the data, not the chant.

The Bathroom Debates

Predator panic on one side, blanket dismissal on the other. What do the incident numbers say? What does the law require? And what does a workable policy look like for a school, a gym, or a church?

There is a practical path. It just isn't on cable news.

The Biology

Is being transgender a real, measurable phenomenon or a social contagion? From brain studies to intersex conditions to hormones in the womb, the biology is real, and it is more complicated than either camp admits.

Written by someone whose own body defies the categories.

You don't have to switch sides. A change of heading of two or three degrees is enough to miss the rocks.

About the author

Who Is Dr. Corinne Votaw-Freer

Intersex · Clinical Psychologist · Christian · Former Conservative Operative

Dr. Corinne Votaw-Freer has lived this debate from more angles than almost anyone writing about it. She was born intersex. She spent years inside conservative politics. She holds a doctorate in psychology and sits with real families as they navigate these questions in real time. And she writes as a Christian who takes both scripture and science seriously.

She has watched both sides distort the evidence, and she wrote this book for the people caught in the middle: parents, teachers, pastors, coaches, and anyone who suspects the truth is more interesting than the talking points.

Read it. Then decide.
